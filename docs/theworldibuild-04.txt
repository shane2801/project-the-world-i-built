This is actually the “magic system” of the entire experience.

The wormholes are not just transitions.

They are the symbol of:

time
growth
transformation
moving between versions of yourself

Around the planet exists a network of glowing paths.

Each major chapter has a wormhole station.

Visual Design

The wormhole should feel:

mysterious
beautiful
technological
emotional

Not like a sci-fi portal.

More like:

“A doorway between memories.”

The Wormhole Station

Each continent has a station.

Example:

France Station

Environment:

Modern city.

The station is:

glass
energy
technology

Inside:

You see fragments of previous worlds.

Small visual memories:

school book
laptop
AI model
project artifacts
Travel Sequence

When the character enters:

Step 1 — Activation

The character approaches.

The portal reacts.

The environment changes.

Lights appear.

Step 2 — Time Tunnel

The character enters.

The tunnel contains:

floating memories.

Examples:

Mauritius travel:

books
school objects
tropical colors

Birmingham:

code

Freelance:

world connections

AI:

Step 3 — Character Transformation

This is one of the strongest ideas.

During travel:

The character changes.

Example:

France → Birmingham:

Current engineer:

mature face
professional clothing

becomes:

younger face
student clothes
laptop backpack

The visitor sees growth visually.

Step 4 — Arrival

The wormhole opens.

The new world appears.

Sound changes.

Lighting changes.

The visitor feels:

"I arrived in another chapter."

Navigation Choice

I think we should avoid making the visitor manually fly everywhere.

Better:

The character has a reason to move.

The system:

Timeline Railway + Wormhole Network

The planet has:

A glowing railway/path around the world.

Each station:

A chapter.

The user can:

walk
explore nearby areas
choose another station

This gives structure.

Why this works

Because a recruiter is not a gamer.

They need guidance.

The wormhole network gives:

Freedom:

“I can explore.”

Structure:

“I know where to go.”

Additional idea:

The wormhole could remember your progress.

Example:

First visit:

You travel normally.

Second visit:

The planet remembers:

Previously explored continents glow.

New projects appear.

The world feels alive.

I would lock this direction:

The wormhole network is the emotional backbone of the planet.
