MASTER CONTEXT — “THE WORLD I BUILT”
Project Vision

Create a scalable interactive 3D portfolio planet that transforms my resume, projects, and career journey into an explorable animated world.

This experience should feel like:

an animated movie
a personal adventure game
a premium professional portfolio

The visitor does not read my career.

The visitor travels through it.

The core emotional idea:

“Explore the world I built throughout my life and career.”

The World Concept

My career exists as a living planet.

This planet represents:

growth
learning
exploration
transformation
things I have created

The planet is not a geographic representation.

It is a symbolic universe where:

continents = life phases
cities = experiences
landmarks = projects
artifacts = achievements
portals = transitions through time

The world should feel alive.
